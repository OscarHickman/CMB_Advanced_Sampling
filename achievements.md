# Achievements — DiffCMB

*Condensed record of what's validated and closed out — current state, not a change log. Full debugging history is in git log. Forward plan: `ROADMAP.md`.*

## Validated foundations

- **Phase 0 — unlensed Gibbs baseline on real Planck data (lmax=300, float64): converged and trusted.** C_ℓ R-hat median 1.026, 0% of multipoles above 1.1; ~48% sampling efficiency. This is the reference everything downstream is checked against.
- **Phase 1 — differentiable lensing operator**: custom TF implementation, gradients w.r.t. both alm and φ validated against finite differences and an independent FD-free gold-standard Jacobian.
- **Phase 1.5 — dense SHT matrix eliminated.** Matrix-free ducc0 SHT behind `tf.custom_gradient`, forward+backward 0.018s at lmax=300 (~500× under the gate; the dense path was 9.38s and didn't fit in GPU memory). CG symmetry/linearity/PD checks pass matrix-free; the lensing op is graph-traceable. This is what makes lmax=300+ tractable at all.
- **HMC + matrix-free-SHT alm sampler — production path, validated.** Replaces the abandoned messenger-field route (below) at ~0.34-0.6s/sweep (~600-1000× cheaper than messenger; ~31× better net ESS/wall-clock throughput than the old dense-SHT reference despite ~4× worse per-sample efficiency). Multi-chain R-hat gate: C_ℓ R-hat max=1.030, median=1.005, 0% above 1.1 — matches or beats the Phase 0 dense-SHT reference. alm-block R-hat is elevated in both (median 1.45-1.60) — expected gradient-noise behaviour at n_alm≈90k, not a regression. Requires MAP initialisation (`--map_steps 2000 --map_lr 0.01 --n_lfs 20`) before burn-in; without it, burn-in has to climb from a naive start and can look like unresolved drift out to l~200-300 even though nothing is actually wrong with the sampler (confirmed by an independent matrix-free-SHT correctness audit: 5.7e-13 relative error vs `healpy.alm2map`, 1.8e-7 on the custom-gradient backward pass — both machine precision).
- **Phase 2 pre-production gates, both resolved.** (1) Joint (alm, φ) mixing at lmax≤50: MARGINAL, not pathological — worst ESS fraction 2.07% (alm block), comparable to Phase 0's accepted single-block ESS at high l. HMC on both blocks, no reparametrisation needed. (2) An unlensed-operator exact-Gaussian Block-2 shortcut for alm|C_ℓ: **rejected** — 652% worst fractional bias in C_l^TT, pervasive across l=2-42 (weak lensing isn't norm-preserving enough at this S/N). HMC-on-both-blocks is the production configuration; do not revisit the shortcut without a fundamentally different exact-draw calibration.
- **Matrix-free port of the Block 3 (φ) lensing operator — done, both full-sky and masked-sky validated.** `lens_map_tf`/`psi_lensed` (`lensing.py`) route through `sht_ducc.py::full_synthesis_tf` instead of the dense Y-matrix, removing the last dense-SHT dependency blocking lmax=300 chains. Full-sky: matches the dense reference to numerical precision (`tests/test_lensing.py`). Masked-sky (f_sky~0.3): matches at phi=0 exactly; at nonzero phi the dense path is *not* a valid masked-sky reference (its zero-padding outside the mask creates an unphysical edge the matrix-free path doesn't have), so masked-sky correctness there is validated by finite differences directly on the matrix-free path (`test_psi_lensed_matrixfree_alm_grad_vs_fd_masked_sky`) — passes on its own terms. lmax=300 smoke run (synthetic full-sky data, warm-started at truth): alm accept 0.860, phi accept 0.520, 53.56s/sweep — confirms the port holds at production scale.
- **Phi-block leapfrog schedule (`phi_n_lfs`) is the parameter that controls φ mixing, not the mass matrix.** Lengthening the trajectory (`phi_n_lfs`: 20→80→200, mass matrix left at default `'prior'`) monotonically improved lag-1 autocorrelation of phi power in all 20/20 seed×multipole combinations tested (mean 0.745→0.576→0.541), with most of the gain already at 80. Production runs use `phi_n_lfs=80` (~5.2× the per-sweep cost of `phi_n_lfs=10`). A Fisher-informed diagonal mass matrix (`estimate_phi_diag_fisher`, opt-in `phi_mass_matrix='fisher'`) is implemented, unit-tested, and correct, but 5-seed smoke evidence showed it makes mixing *worse* on average (mean L=2 autocorrelation 0.383→0.495, 3/5 seeds regressed) — closed as unfavorable, not pursued further. Do not re-attempt without new evidence that the one-shot Fisher curvature estimate isn't itself too noisy/state-dependent to help.
- **Beam transfer function + HEALPix pixel window — validated, opt-in.** `power.py::beam_pixwin_transfer(lmax, fwhm_arcmin, nside)` threaded through `CosmologyAdvancedSampling(..., beam_fwhm_arcmin=None)` (`None` = old behaviour, zero effect on existing call sites), applied as a diagonal multiply on the unlensed alm in the likelihood, lensing operator, and lensed-φ posterior (not the φ prior, which is correctly beam-independent). Validated against an independent ground truth (`healpy.almxfl`+`healpy.alm2map`, not the model's own machinery).
- **Anisotropic per-pixel noise N_ii — validated, opt-in.** `noise_map=None` kwarg on `CosmologyAdvancedSampling.__init__` builds a spatially-varying `self.Ninv` in place of the uniform scalar; `None` preserves old behaviour bit-for-bit. Validated against an independent ground truth, isolating the likelihood term via a Ninv-zeroed twin model. Both realism features together: full suite passes, no regressions.
- **Block 4 — C_L^φφ|φ exact inverse-Gamma draw — validated, opt-in (`sample_cl_phiphi=True`).** Same exact-conjugacy argument as Block 1's C_ℓ|alm draw, applied to φ's Gaussian prior term. Validated against an independent brute-force healpy-ordering weighting cross-check and a 3000-realization recovery test at known constant C_L^φφ (matches the bias-adjusted expected InvGamma posterior mean to within 6 standard errors). Resamples `cl_phiphi_full` every sweep and rebuilds the φ HMC mass matrix to match; mutually exclusive with `phi_mass_matrix='fisher'` (a frozen-at-burn-in mass matrix would go stale against a spectrum that changes every sweep). Full checkpoint save/resume support, including backward-compatible loading of pre-Block-4 checkpoints.
- **φ-block memory leak — root-caused and fixed.** Eager-mode `tf.py_function` calls (the φ HMC step, forced eager because deflection-geometry recompute isn't traceable through plain TF ops) register a callback in TF's process-global eager py-function registry on every call and are never freed — a documented TF footgun, not a ducc0/geometry resource leak as first suspected. Fixed by tracing `bootstrap_results`+`one_step` together in `@tf.function` for both the alm and phi HMC steps. The one subtlety: `log_prob_phi_block`'s `cl_phiphi_full` argument (Block 4's per-sweep-resampled spectrum) had to be promoted from a closed-over numpy array to a `tf.Variable` read via `tf.convert_to_tensor`, or tracing would have silently frozen the φ-prior spectrum at its first-trace value — a correctness bug, not just a missed optimization. Verified: RSS is now flat over long runs (was ~362 MB/sweep at `phi_n_lfs=80`, lmax=32; now ~17 KB/sweep of allocator noise — ~20,000× reduction). This unblocks all lmax=300 φ-active production chains.
- **lmax=300 point-validation run at `phi_n_lfs=80` (post-leak-fix): completed, but with an open φ-amplitude caveat.** The run itself is healthy — alm accept 0.705, phi accept 0.643, no frozen-block signature — but the recovered φ power is systematically **under**-estimated by roughly half to seven-eighths across l=10-300 (worst z=−7.86 at l=[30,60)); the alm/C_ℓ^TT blocks are clean. Not yet root-caused; candidate explanations (insufficient post-resume burn-in re-equilibration, a genuine amplitude bug, checkpoint-stitching drift across the multi-job resume history) are unconfirmed. **Standing decision: not chased with further lmax=300 compute** — the lmax≈128 coverage-pilot track (see `ROADMAP.md`) is the primary exactness evidence and doesn't depend on resolving this. This result stands as a demonstrated-scale point check with its bias caveat stated plainly.

## Coverage-test prerequisite findings (lmax≈128 pilot track)

- **Strict simulation-based calibration only applies to the fields (alm, φ), not the spectra (C_ℓ, C_L^φφ).** Blocks 1 and 4's exact conditionals are structurally identical to their likelihoods (`C_ℓ|alm ~ InvGamma(ℓ-0.5, S_ℓ/2)` has the same functional form as the C_ℓ likelihood itself), so the implied prior on each spectrum is flat and improper — there is no `θ_true ~ p(θ)` to rank. The spectra instead get an interval-coverage check against *realized* power (never the CAMB ensemble mean — see the cosmic-variance bug below), which is a weaker claim and must be labelled as coverage rather than calibration in any figure caption. Documented in `scripts/aggregate_coverage_ranks.py`'s header.
- **A cold alm start can freeze the φ block outright — avoided by starting from a data-driven MAP estimate.** A cold independent-prior alm start left a large residual that the φ block absorbed by inflating its low-L amplitude; Block 4 then tracked that inflated φ and rebuilt the φ mass matrix from it every sweep, locking φ at near-zero accept — self-reinforcing, no route back. This did not show up in Block 4's own truth-warm-started validation, since a truth start has no residual to run away from — a reminder that truth-warm-started validation cannot certify cold-start behaviour. Fixed: pilot chains now start from `find_map_estimate` (data-driven, never sees `alm_true`/`phi_true`, so it's a legitimate calibration-test start) with a raised burn-in, plus a first-class φ accept-rate floor (0.15) in the pilot's verdict logic ahead of any trace statistics.
- **Coverage harness is built and smoke-tested, not yet launched.** `scripts/coverage_ensemble_chain.py` (one realization per SLURM array task), `scripts/aggregate_coverage_ranks.py` (offline rank pooling + KS uniformity), `scripts/submit_coverage_ensemble.slurm` (array launcher). Launch is gated on the pilot chain returning GO and `--thin` being set from its measured lag-k autocorrelation — see `ROADMAP.md` for current pilot status.
- **The joint (C_ℓ^TT, C_L^φφ) correlation-figure question is open, not answered.** A single-chain pilot analysis (`scripts/analyze_joint_posterior_pilot.py`, bins both spectra into 5 l-ranges, computes the Pearson correlation matrix on ln-power) found all correlations small (|r| ≤ 0.088), but the pilot chain's own lag-1 autocorrelation (~0.97-0.98) implies only ~5-10 effective independent draws in 600 raw sweeps — the expected sampling noise at that N_eff (~0.3-0.45) swamps every value measured. This cannot yet distinguish "no interesting joint structure" from "underpowered." Needs either a longer single chain or pooled traces from the eventual coverage ensemble (whose own justification doesn't depend on this figure).

## Real bugs found and fixed (dense-reference validation catches, not derivation trust)

- **φ-block HMC gradient**: an FD backward pass could re-query a shifted angle into a different bilinear-interpolation cell, producing wild step-size-dependent gradients — replaced with an analytic bilinear-interpolation gradient. Block 3 gradients are production-trustworthy.
- **alm precision convention**: uniform 1/C_ℓ used where the real spherical-harmonic convention requires 2/C_ℓ for m>0 — a genuine variance error (not the cause of any sampler stall).
- **Messenger conditional derivation**: the plausible-looking naive `t|s,d` precision was subtly wrong (needs the reduced noise N−T); caught against an exact dense reference before touching production.
- **SHT operator normalisation**: AᵀA is not a scalar multiple of identity — needs per-mode m=0/m>0 weights *and* empirical calibration (HEALPix quadrature is ~1-2% off the analytic constant, enough to bias specific modes by tens of posterior SEs).
- **HMC + matrix-free SHT JIT-compile incompatibility**: `jit_compile=True` cannot compile through `tf.py_function`, so the combination had silently never run — a one-line fix (`model.py::psi_tf` disables JIT when `use_matrixfree_sht=True`) unlocked production HMC.
- **`tf.gather`/`IndexedSlices` on a custom-gradient output**: `tf.gather` on a `tf.custom_gradient` op's output produces `IndexedSlices`, not a dense tensor, as the upstream cotangent — any `tf.py_function`/`.numpy()` call inside that op's backward pass must `tf.convert_to_tensor()` it first. Applies to any future custom-gradient op feeding into `tf.gather`/`tf.boolean_mask`.
- **φ-block memory leak (eager `tf.py_function` registration)**: see above. Root cause of the lmax=300 OOM saga; a stale "cannot be traced" code comment had gone unrevisited after the matrix-free SHT path made it false — re-verify such assumptions against current code before trusting them.
- **Cosmic variance mistaken for sampler bias**: an early simulation-validation script compared posterior C_ℓ^TT draws against the CAMB *ensemble-average* input spectrum, producing spurious multi-sigma "bias" at low l from ordinary cosmic variance. Fixed by comparing against the *realized* power in the actual drawn alm_true (`model.compute_sl_np`) instead.
- **`integrated_autocorr_time`'s zero-variance short-circuit was a hidden absolute-tolerance bug**: `np.allclose(x, 0.0)` used to guard against constant input, but phi-power summary statistics are naturally O(1e-9)-O(1e-17) in absolute magnitude — well inside `np.allclose`'s default `atol=1e-8` — so real fluctuations were misread as "no variance" and silently reported as fake ESS=100%. Fixed with a scale-relative check (`np.allclose(x / np.abs(x).max(), 0.0)`) in both gate scripts affected. Lesson: a "no variance" fallback needs a tolerance scaled to the data, not a hardcoded absolute one.

## Closed-out sampler routes (do not revisit without new evidence)

- **Diagonally-preconditioned CG on the masked sky: abandoned.** The Jacobi preconditioner degrades ~4 orders of magnitude under a realistic mask (f_sky≈0.74); extrapolated cost 10⁴-10⁵ iterations per Gibbs step.
- **Messenger-field sampler: built, validated at small scale, closed as non-viable at production scale.** Correct conditionals validated against dense references; a masked-sky divergence traced to off-diagonal AᵀA coupling and fixed with a block-diagonal-by-m correction. The production run completed but failed validation with intrinsic critical slowing down at n_alm≈90k (ESS 0.6%, C_ℓ biased low, monotonic drift). Ruled out as fixes: more inner iterations (~25-85 hours/sweep), τ² annealing, mask topology, C_ℓ dynamic range, the block approximation itself (the exact dense correction mixes equally slowly). The slowdown is dimensionality-intrinsic to plain messenger Gibbs — the known failure mode motivating messenger-as-CG-preconditioner work in the literature. Superseded by the HMC + matrix-free-SHT pivot above.
- **Unlensed-operator exact Block-2 shortcut for alm|C_ℓ: rejected**, see Phase 2 pre-production gates above. Not fundamentally dead — a CG solve built from autodiff of `psi_lensed` (mirroring `_cg_grad_fn`'s use of `_psi_tf_raw`) could target the correct lensed conditional exactly, but is unexplored and not worth pursuing unless HMC-on-both-blocks becomes a proven throughput bottleneck (currently passes MARGINAL, no urgency).

Since Block 4 landed, the sampler technically spans (alm_unlensed, C_ℓ, φ, C_L^φφ) — whether to widen the headline claim to name C_L^φφ is an open drafting-time decision (see `ROADMAP.md`); don't let the two documents drift apart on this.
